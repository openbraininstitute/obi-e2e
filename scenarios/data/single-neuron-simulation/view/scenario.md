# Single neuron (legacy) details

Opening one Single neuron (legacy) from its listing, under the simulations section.

A row opens a small panel beside the listing. That panel has a way through to
the full page, which is where the whole record lives.

No Single neuron (legacy) exists on either deployment yet, so both cases skip until one does.
This is one of the legacy simulations, which does not use the scan-configuration
editor, so nothing is claimed here about what its page holds beyond opening.

User: authenticated

## Open one Single neuron (legacy) beside the listing

Precondition:

1. On the Single neuron (legacy) listing, with at least one result

Steps:

1. Click a result

Expected:

- Its name is showing
- These properties are showing:
  "Circuit", "Status", "Registration date" and "Lifecycle status"
- It offers a download
- It offers a way through to its full details

## Open the full Single neuron (legacy) page

Precondition:

1. One Single neuron (legacy) is open beside the listing

Steps:

1. Choose to see its details

Expected:

- The Single neuron (legacy)'s own page is showing
