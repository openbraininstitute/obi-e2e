# The atlas and its neuron counts

The 3D view on the Data page, and the figures beside it: how many neurons the
chosen region holds, and what they are made up of.

The count can be read two ways — as a number of neurons, or as a density — and a
switch moves between them.

User: authenticated

## The 3D view opens with the region's neuron count

Precondition:

1. Inside my project, on the Data page

Steps:

1. Look at the 3D view

Expected:

- The 3D view is showing
- The neuron count for the chosen region is showing
- The count is a number
- There is a "Reset camera" button

## The switch turns the count into a density

Precondition:

1. On the Data page

Steps:

1. Note the neuron count
2. Turn on the count and density switch

Expected:

- The switch is on
- The figure shown is no longer the count that was noted

## The switch turns the density back into a count

After: The switch turns the count into a density

Steps:

1. Turn the switch off again

Expected:

- The switch is off
- The figure shown is the count that was noted

## The region is broken down by cell type

Precondition:

1. On the Data page

Steps:

1. Look at the breakdown beside the 3D view

Expected:

- It is headed "M-TYPES"
- At least one cell type is listed
- Every cell type listed carries a figure
