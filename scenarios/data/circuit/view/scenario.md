# Circuit details

Opening one Circuit from its listing, under the models section.

A row opens a small panel beside the listing. That panel has a way through to
the full page, which is where the whole record lives.

User: lab member

## Open one Circuit beside the listing

Precondition:

1. On the Circuit listing

Steps:

1. Click a result

Expected:

- Its name is showing
- These properties are showing:
  "species", "brain_region", "scale"
  "number_neurons", "number_synapses"
  "number_connections", "build_category", "creation_date"
  "license", "lifecycle_status"
- It offers a download
- It offers a way through to its full details

## Open the full Circuit page

Precondition:

1. One Circuit is open beside the listing

Steps:

1. Choose to see its details

Expected:

- The Circuit's own page is showing
- These parts of the page are showing:
  "visualizations", "visualization", "metadata-grid"
  "subject-details"
