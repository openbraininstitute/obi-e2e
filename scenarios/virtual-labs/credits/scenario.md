# Project credits

What the lab and the project hold, what has been spent, and the two ways to put
more in.

Buying and transferring credits move real money, so these tests read the page
and leave the balances alone.

User: authenticated

## See what the lab and the project hold

Precondition:

1. Inside my project

Steps:

1. Open the Credits page

Expected:

- The "Virtual lab credits" figure is showing
- The "Project credits" figure is showing
- Both are amounts

## The two ways to add credits are offered

Precondition:

1. On the Credits page

Steps:

1. Look at what the page offers

Expected:

- There is a "Buy credits" button
- There is a "Transfer credits" button
- There is a "Pricing" button

## Pricing opens the public plans in a new tab

Precondition:

1. On the Credits page

Steps:

1. Click "Pricing"

Expected:

- A new tab opens on the page headed "Pricing"
- The Credits page is still open behind it

## The history says what every credit went on

Precondition:

1. On the Credits page

Steps:

1. Go to the "History" section

Expected:

- The columns are "Category", "Type", "Member", "Date" and "Cost (Credits)"
- Once the project has spent something, an entry is listed for it
- The entry gives a cost
- The history can be paged
