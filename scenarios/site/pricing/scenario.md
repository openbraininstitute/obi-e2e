# Pricing page

The four plans, what each one costs, and how to ask about the two that are
quoted rather than priced.

User: visitor
Page: /pricing

## Open the Pricing page

Steps:

1. Open the Pricing page

Expected:

- The "Pricing" headline is showing
- The text under it mentions flexible subscription plans
- There is a "Discover our plans" button

## All four plans are offered

Precondition:

1. On the Pricing page, at the plans

Steps:

1. Look at the plans

Expected:

- There are four plans
- "Free" is one of them
- "Pro" is one of them
- "Enterprise" is one of them
- "Education" is one of them

## The Pro plan shows a price in Swiss francs

Precondition:

1. On the Pricing page, at the plans

Steps:

1. Look at the "Pro" plan

Expected:

- Its price is given in "CHF"
- It offers a "Monthly" and a "Yearly" price

## Enterprise and Education are quoted on request

Precondition:

1. On the Pricing page, at the plans

Steps:

1. Look at the "Enterprise" plan, then the "Education" plan

Expected:

- Each one offers a "Contact Us" link
- Each link opens a message to the subscription address
