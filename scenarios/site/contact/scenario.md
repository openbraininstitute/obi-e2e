# Contact page

The two addresses a visitor can write to: one for support, one for anything
else.

User: visitor
Page: /contact

## Open the Contact page

Steps:

1. Open the Contact page

Expected:

- The "Contact" headline is showing
- There is a "Need Help? Get in Touch!" button

## Both contact addresses are offered

Precondition:

1. On the Contact page

Steps:

1. Look at the ways to get in touch

Expected:

- There are two of them
- One writes to the support address
- One writes to the general enquiry address
