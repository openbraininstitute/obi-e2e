# About page

What the institute is, where it came from, and who helped build it. A public
page, so a visitor sees all of it.

User: visitor
Page: /about

## Open the About page

Steps:

1. Open the About page

Expected:

- The "About" headline is showing
- The text under it says the Open Brain Institute is a non-profit organization
- There is a "Discover our origin" button
- The hero image has loaded

## The page tells the institute's story in sections

Precondition:

1. On the About page

Steps:

1. Read down the page

Expected:

- There is a "2025: The Open Brain Institute" section
- There is an "Our Foundations: Blue Brain" section
- There is a "We thank all Blue Brain collaborators and contributors" section
- Each of those sections has text under its heading

## Browse our portals lists the legacy portals

Precondition:

1. On the About page

Steps:

1. Go to the "Browse our portals" section

Expected:

- There are eight portal cards
- Every card has a title
- Every card links somewhere

## Load more shows more contributors

Precondition:

1. On the About page, at the contributors list

Steps:

1. Note how many contributors are named
2. Click "Load more"

Expected:

- More contributors are named than were noted
