# Site footer

The foot of every public page: the links, the social accounts, the copyright,
and the newsletter form.

User: visitor
Page: /

## The footer names the institute and the year

Steps:

1. Open the home page
2. Go to the foot of the page

Expected:

- The footer carries the "Open Brain Institute" name
- The copyright line reads "Copyright © 2026 - Open Brain Institute"

## The footer links reach every public page

Precondition:

1. At the foot of the home page

Steps:

1. Look at the links

Expected:

- There are twelve of them
- They are "About OBI", "Our story", "Mission", "Team", "Gallery", "Pricing", "News", "Contact", "Login", "Terms and conditions", "Financing policy" and "Privacy policy"

## The footer links to all four social accounts

Precondition:

1. At the foot of the home page

Steps:

1. Look at the social links

Expected:

- There are four of them
- One goes to LinkedIn
- One goes to X
- One goes to YouTube
- One goes to Bluesky

## Subscribing needs an address and the privacy box

Precondition:

1. At the foot of the home page, on the "Subscribe to our newsletter" form

Steps:

1. Look at the "Subscribe" button
2. Type "e2e@example.com" into the address box
3. Tick the privacy box

Expected:

- "Subscribe" starts out unavailable
- The address box holds "e2e@example.com" once typed
- The privacy box is ticked
- "Subscribe" is available once both are done
