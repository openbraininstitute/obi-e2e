# Site navigation

The top menu on every public page. Two of its entries open a submenu; the rest
are links.

User: visitor
Page: /

## The top menu offers the same entries everywhere

Steps:

1. Open the home page

Expected:

- There is an "About" menu
- There is a "The Platform" menu
- There is a "News" link
- There is a "Contact" link
- There is a "Login" link

## The About menu reaches each of its pages

Precondition:

1. On the home page

Steps:

1. Open the "About" menu
2. Follow each entry in turn, coming back to the home page after each one

Expected:

- "About OBI" opens the page headed "About"
- "Our story" opens the page headed "The Real Digital Brain Story"
- "Mission" opens the page headed "Mission"
- "Team" opens the page headed "Team"

## The Platform menu reaches each of its pages

Precondition:

1. On the home page

Steps:

1. Open the "The Platform" menu
2. Follow each entry in turn, coming back to the home page after each one

Expected:

- "Features" opens the page headed "Features"
- "Showcases" opens the page headed "Showcases"
- "Pricing" opens the page headed "Pricing"

## Login asks the visitor to sign in

Precondition:

1. On the home page

Steps:

1. Click "Login"

Expected:

- The sign-in page says "Log in or sign up"
- It offers to sign in with GitHub
- It offers to sign in with Google
- It offers to sign in with Microsoft
