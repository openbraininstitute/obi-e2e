# Mission page

What the institute set out to do, and the mission statement a visitor can
download.

User: visitor
Page: /mission

## Open the Mission page

Steps:

1. Open the Mission page

Expected:

- The "Mission" headline is showing
- The headline under it says the mission is to empower researchers and organizations
- The hero image has loaded

## The page names the four things a virtual lab is for

Precondition:

1. On the Mission page

Steps:

1. Read down the page

Expected:

- "Explore" is one of them
- "Build" is one of them
- "Experiment" is one of them
- "Collaborate" is one of them
