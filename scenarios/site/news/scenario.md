# News page

The institute's announcements, newest first, ten at a time.

User: visitor
Page: /news

## Open the News page

Steps:

1. Open the News page

Expected:

- The "News" headline is showing
- Ten articles are listed

## Every article is titled, pictured and can be opened

Precondition:

1. On the News page

Steps:

1. Look at the ten articles

Expected:

- Every one of them has a title with text
- Every one of them offers a "Read more" link
- There is an image for every one of them

## Load more articles adds to the list

Precondition:

1. On the News page

Steps:

1. Note that ten articles are listed
2. Click "Load 10 more articles"

Expected:

- More than ten articles are listed
