# Paired neurons simulation

The film a scientist watches to learn the workflow: the Workflows page, the
Simulate category, the Paired neurons card, the circuit, the form, the campaign
generated, launched and followed to its traces. One take, as the scenario's
cases would run them one after the other, with time to see each page.

Pace: 2 seconds
Size: 1920x1080
User: credits
Seed: seed.json

## The Workflows page

Steps:

1. Open the Workflows page of my project
2. Choose "Simulate"
3. Start "Paired neurons"

Show:

- The circuit listing, with the search box

## The circuit

Steps:

1. Search for the circuit the seed names and pick it
2. Press "Use model"

Show:

- The Paired neurons simulation form

## The form

Steps:

1. Fill the form from the configuration in the seed

Show:

- "Generate simulation(s)" enabled

## The campaign

Steps:

1. Press "Generate simulation(s)"
2. Go back to the configuration tab, then to the simulations tab
3. Press "Launch simulations", then "Confirm"

Show:

- One coordinate, read "created", then leaving it
- The coordinate reaching "done", with "Recording 0" and
  "S1nonbarrel_neurons" among its outputs

## The results

Steps:

1. Open "Recording 0"
2. Open "S1nonbarrel_neurons"

Show:

- The traces of "2 cells recorded"
- The spikes of "PopulationS1nonbarrel_neurons", held for one pace at the end
